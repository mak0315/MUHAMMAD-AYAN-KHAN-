## 4. Cannibalization and keyword-stuffing rules

- Keep the generic brand term **"Muhammad Ayan Khan"** led by the homepage and About page; other pages should use it as a qualifier, not as their primary target.
- Do not repeat the same primary keyword on two pages.
- Use each primary keyword naturally in the title, one heading, and the first paragraph — not in every paragraph.
- Never repeat the homepage's existing meta description on a new page.
- Do not add keywords to image alt text unless the image genuinely matches the description.
- Avoid unverified superlatives (for example, "first", "best", "leading") and numeric claims until they can be evidenced.
